# Spooky Neighbors — Targeted Visual QA Corrections

This is a targeted correction pass, not another full prompt experiment. Full resolution review of v1 identified eight pages with specific, correctable issues. Original accepted pages 01, 06, 08, 09, 10, 13, and 14 are intentionally excluded from regeneration.

## Replace original page 02: Patchwork Monster Builds a Birdhouse

**Observed defect:** Correct the robot-like face from v1: the main character must read as a soft stitched creature, not a gadget. Keep the birdhouse opening colorable and its support relationship obvious.

**Correction plan:** Stitched creature face, birdhouse entrance is outlined with a white interior, bird feet on roof, hammer resting on table, broad pumpkin shapes, no electronic-looking screen face.

**Acceptance criteria:** The scene is understandable at thumbnail size; major surfaces remain white and colorable; supports and object relationships read clearly; any remaining fine detail belongs to the main activity rather than being texture filler.

## Batch acceptance gate

A revised page only replaces the original if it fixes its named defect without introducing a new major composition/anatomy issue. If a candidate still fails, keep the existing image temporarily and regenerate only that page using its specific QA notes. No packaging or listing publication until the final selected 15-page set passes review.
