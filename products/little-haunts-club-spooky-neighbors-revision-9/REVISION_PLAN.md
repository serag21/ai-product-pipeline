# Spooky Neighbors — Targeted Visual QA Corrections

This is a targeted correction pass. The first 15-page set has a consistent illustration language and several strong pages, but nine pages have specific defects that warrant regeneration. The other six original pages are deliberately left alone.

## Original page 02: Patchwork Monster Builds a Birdhouse

**Design rationale:** Correct the robot-like face from v1: the main character must read as a soft stitched creature, not a gadget. Keep the birdhouse opening colorable and its support relationship obvious.

**Targeted review:** Stitched creature face, birdhouse entrance is outlined with a white interior, bird feet on roof, hammer resting on table, broad pumpkin shapes, no electronic-looking screen face.

**Acceptance gate:** The main action reads at thumbnail size; large interiors remain white and colorable; props are supported/attached/held plausibly; the scene has a single visual focal point; there are no distracting anatomy or rendering errors. A technically successful ComfyUI run is not a visual pass.
