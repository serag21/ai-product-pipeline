## Patchwork Monster's Birdhouse Workshop — no winner

- **FIX2-A / lab page 01:** clearest stitched-monster character, but the image introduced colored green grass and orange pumpkins. It is not a monochrome coloring-page candidate as rendered. The birdhouse entrance is also too dark.
- **FIX2-B / lab page 02:** clean room composition, but character reads as an ordinary human child, not the requested patchwork monster.
- **FIX2-C / lab page 03:** character looks cat-like; birdhouse entrance is filled black and the scale/prop interaction is awkward.

Decision: none promoted. One final set of three prompts front-loads the monochrome line-art requirement and reinforces the stitched-cloth character positively.

## Scarecrow's Bird Friend — provisional winner: FIX11-A / lab page 04

- **FIX11-A / lab page 04:** best balance of a friendly scarecrow, a connected fence, one pumpkin, two flowers and a watering can. Crow has a mostly black body but useful white feather panels. Unlike the earlier scarecrow pages, pumpkins don't dominate the scene.
- **FIX11-B / lab page 05:** better birdhouse/feeding action but the overall character pose and sparse scene are weaker; the crow remains mostly black.
- **FIX11-C / lab page 06:** interesting nest-in-tree relationship, but a large black crow and nest texture add more visual weight than needed.

Decision: use FIX11-A provisionally. Small black features can be acceptable; this crow's white wing panel gives it a meaningful coloring region. Recheck its full-resolution page during final page QA.
